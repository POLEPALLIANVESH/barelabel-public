BareLabel — Privacy Policy

Review status: Reviewed by the operator on 2026-06-07. Informational only — BareLabel does not give medical advice. Consult a qualified professional (allergist, dietitian, or physician) before acting on any ingredient score, especially for food allergens.

Effective date: to be set on first publication

Operator: BareLabel (the "Service"). Contact: privacy@barelabel.app.

1. Summary

We make a mobile app that scores food, cosmetic, and household-product ingredient labels. We collect the minimum data needed to do that: your account ID (from your sign-in provider), your allergen preferences (only if you choose to set them), your scan history (so we can show it back to you), and basic operational telemetry. We do not sell your data. You can export everything we hold and delete your account at any time, from inside the app.

BareLabel is informational. Scores reflect publicly available ingredient data and our scoring rules; they do not constitute medical, nutritional, or safety advice. Consult a qualified professional before acting on any score, especially if you have a known allergy, intolerance, or medical condition.

3. Why we collect it

  • To render personalized ingredient-safety scores (allergen preferences + scan history).
  • To sync your state across your devices (account identifier + scan history).
  • To keep the service available and abuse-free (telemetry).
  • To show ads — only if you opt in and ads are enabled.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access / export your data — in-app: Profile → "Export my data". You receive a download link by email within 24 hours (legal SLA: 30 days).
  • Delete your account — in-app: Profile → "Delete account". Your data is soft-deleted immediately; permanent purge runs within 30 days.
  • Correct your data — edit your allergen preferences and account email in-app at any time.
  • Object to processing — disable telemetry in Settings; disable ads in Settings.
  • Restrict processing — sign out (no further data is collected until you sign back in).
  • Withdraw consent — toggle off allergen preferences, ads, or analytics in Settings. Withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority. For EU residents: see https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

EU/UK users have the right to data portability — the in-app export delivers a machine-readable JSON archive.

California residents have additional rights under CCPA / CPRA: right to know, right to delete, right to correct, right to opt-out of sale/sharing (which we honor automatically), right to non-discrimination, and right to limit use of sensitive personal information.

7.1 California (CCPA / CPRA) — Sensitive Personal Information

Allergen preferences and scan history that reveal a consumer's health condition or medical history qualify as Sensitive Personal Information (SPI) under the CPRA. BareLabel uses SPI solely to provide the core requested service — rendering your personalized ingredient score on-device. We do not use SPI to infer characteristics about you, to build advertising profiles, for cross-context behavioral advertising, or for any secondary purpose. Because our use is limited to the permitted purposes under CPRA §1798.121(a), no separate "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" control is required; you may still delete all SPI at any time via Profile → Delete account.

7.2 California (CCPA) — 12-month historical disclosure

In the preceding 12 months, BareLabel collected the categories of personal information listed in Section 2 above, for the purposes described in Section 3, from the sources described in Section 2, and shared them only with the sub-processors listed in Section 5. We did not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. International data transfers

For EU/UK users, primary processing is in EU (Azure northeurope). When data is transferred outside the EEA (e.g., to a US-based identity provider when you sign in with Apple/Google/Microsoft), the transfer relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses + Google's / Apple's / Microsoft's certifications under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where applicable).

9. Security

We use TLS 1.3 for all transit, encryption at rest, Managed Identity for all internal data-plane access (no shared keys), Ed25519 signatures on every rule set and dictionary update the app downloads, certificate pinning in the mobile app, and Play Integrity / App Attest tamper detection. We follow OWASP MASVS L2 + OWASP API Top-10. Responsible disclosure: security@barelabel.app.

10. Children

BareLabel is not directed at children under 13 (US, per COPPA) or under 16 (EU, per GDPR-K). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact privacy@barelabel.app and we will delete it.

Although BareLabel is intended for adults (including parents who scan products on behalf of their children), the app's onboarding requires the account holder to confirm they are an adult before allergen preferences can be enabled. Allergen profiles are stored under the adult account holder; the app does not expose a separate child-profile feature, and we do not knowingly accept accounts created by minors. If a child has set up an account or had allergen data logged under their name, contact privacy@barelabel.app and we will delete the account and all associated data.

11. Changes to this policy

We notify users of material changes via in-app notification at least 30 days before they take effect. The current version + effective date is always available at https://barelabel.app/privacy and in-app under Settings → Legal.
